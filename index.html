Sells the home at the configured age: proceeds minus selling costs, minus federal LTCG tax on the gain above the Section 121 principal-residence exclusion ($250k single / $500k married), are injected into the taxable account as new basis and flow through the standard withdrawal waterfall. 🏛️ Estate & Legacy (SECURE Act 2.0 heir model)

Non-eligible designated beneficiaries (most adult children) must drain an inherited pre-tax IRA within 10 years of death — modeled as even annual drains taxed at the heir's own bracket. Roth inheritances stay tax-free, which is exactly where the Roth-conversion arbitrage pays off for your heirs. Spouses, minor children and disabled heirs are exempt from the 10-year rule.

🎢 Dynamic Glidepath (bond tent)

Equity exposure ramps down to 80% across the ramp years before retirement (sequence-of-returns protection), then glides back to 100% over the re-risking window. Blends every risky account's return toward the cash/bond return; dampens volatility in Monte Carlo too.

📍 Asset Location Tax-Drag Optimizer

Same investments, smarter placement: dividend-heavy / high-turnover assets belong in pre-tax (taxed later as ordinary income), max-growth equity belongs in Roth (appreciation never taxed), leaving taxable for tax-efficient growth. Drag is measured on retirement-average balances at your effective qualified-dividend rate.

Dividend yield % is applied to the taxable brokerage balance each year (default 2.5%): the cash is taxed at qualified-dividend / LTCG rates (0/15/20% + 3.8% NIIT over $200k MAGI, not ordinary rates), stacks into the income waterfall, and any excess over spending needs is reinvested into the taxable account.

🌉 Phased Retirement / Bridge Income

Salary-replacement cash flow for the first retirement years — consulting, part-time work, severity payouts — injected ahead of pension and Social Security to cut early-year portfolio withdrawals and sequence risk.

🏠 Mortgage Payoff vs. Invested Arbitrage

Payments are fixed nominal debt service added to annual needs. The comparison runs your plan both ways — carry the loan vs. liquidate at retirement — and shows the net estate delta.

🏦 HECM Reverse-Mortgage Volatility Buffer

Standby reverse mortgage: the untapped credit line compounds every year, and in bear-market years (negative equity returns) portfolio withdrawals are intercepted and funded tax-free from the LOC instead — the portfolio keeps its compounding base and no shares are sold at the bottom.

❤️ Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) Engine (70½+)

direct pre-tax → charity · excluded from income & MAGI · counts toward the RMD (cap ≈ $105k/yr)

For retirees who give to charity anyway, the QCD route satisfies the gift with pre-tax dollars that never appear on your 1040 — shrinking RMDs, taxable income and IRMAA tiers simultaneously.

⏳ IRS Rule 72(t) SEPP (early-withdrawal program)

Amortization method: balance ÷ annuity factor over your life expectancy at the IRS reasonable rate — a fixed annual pre-tax draw, penalty-free, locked for the longer of 5 years or until age 59½. The exact safe-harbor amount overrides the standard waterfall for those years.

🧾 HSA "Shoebox" Strategy (delayed reimbursement)

medical paid from the taxable/cash waterfall; HSA stays fully invested

Every out-of-pocket medical receipt banks into a running tax-free reimbursement credit. From the chosen age the HSA pays itself back — tax-free cash that can cover any purpose, since qualified expenses were already satisfied by the shoeboxed receipts.

on · tax-free

🩺 Form SSA-44 IRMAA Work-Stoppage Waiver (life-changing event)

IRMAA tiers are priced off MAGI from two years earlier — so the first Medicare years after a career exit still "see" your final full salary. Filing Form SSA-44 (Work Stoppage / retirement) makes SSA re-determine tiers on projected current-year income instead.

🎢 Retirement Spending Curve (go-go / slow-go / no-go)

Real-world retirees don't spend flat-inflation: the go-go years run hot, real spending phases down through the slow-go middle, then late-life healthcare and support needs lift it again.

🪣 One-Off Bucket List Expense

A one-time splurge — the world cruise, the safari — drawn through the withdrawal waterfall at a chosen age. It never permanently raises the inflation-adjusted baseline spending level or triggers guardrail cuts.

Withdrawal Strategy Engine

—

Fixed SWR: the initial retirement withdrawal is set by the spending plan and then adjusted strictly for inflation every year — the classic Bengen / Trinity study approach.

Guardrail band20%

Capital Preservation: if the current withdrawal rate rises more than this far above the initial rate, spending is cut 10% (guards against sequence-of-returns risk).

Guardrail step (cut / raise size)10%

Prosperity Rule: if the current withdrawal rate drops more than the band below the initial rate, spending is raised by this step — cuts and raises are flagged in the ledger & charts.

VPW recalculates the withdrawal each year as balance ÷ annuity factor(remaining life expectancy, real return). If the formula allows less than the spending plan needs, discretionary spending flexes down to the funded level (flagged VPW CUT); surplus years stay invested.

📈 CAPE Valuation Adjuster (Shiller cyclically-adjusted P/E)

High starting valuations historically precede lower 30-yr real returns: above a CAPE of 30 the safe withdrawal level tapers toward −28%, below 20 it rises up to +18%. Applied to every retirement-year spending level, on top of any guardrail strategy.

Withdrawal Waterfall Order

Each year, after income (salary, pension, Social Security, annuity, rental) and HSA medical coverage, any funding gap is drawn in this order. RMDs from pre-tax accounts begin at 75 and are injected as income; Roth conversions (if enabled) run first from pre-tax. When the 3-Bucket engine is enabled it replaces this order in retirement with bucket-draw + refill logic. Adjust balances/flows above and the ledger + charts recompute instantly.

💹 0% Capital-Gains Harvesting Optimizer

—

In low-income years the federal 0% LTCG bracket (up to $48,350 of taxable income, single filer) is often completely unused. This engine sells just enough appreciated taxable lots — and instantly rebuys them — to fill that room: cash-flow neutral, $0 federal tax now, cost basis steps up so future withdrawals are taxed on less gain. Room = 0% top − ordinary taxable income − qualified dividends − gains already realized from withdrawals. State income tax on realized gains still applies.

Share of the taxable balance that is appreciation (basis = 1 − share). Drives both harvest capacity and gain-only taxation of withdrawals.

📉 Tax-Loss Harvesting & Carryforward

In down markets the engine realizes unrealized lot losses from the taxable account (sell & rebuy, no wash-sale modeling), offsets realized gains dollar-for-dollar, then up to $3,000/yr against ordinary income — the rest carries forward indefinitely and steps future basis down honestly.

🎗️ Charitable Bunching / Donor-Advised Fund

The household gives every year either way — bunching groups N years of gifts (e.g. $50k every 5 years) into a single DAF contribution that pushes past the standard deduction and forces itemizing that year, while the skipped years take the standard deduction at $0 marginal cost. Only the deduction timing changes; total giving is identical.

🏛️ Tax Policy Shift / TCJA Sunset (2026+ scenario)

Reverts federal ordinary brackets to the pre-TCJA structure — 10% · 15% · 25% · 28% · 33% · 35% · 39.6% — and halves the standard deduction ($15,000 → $7,500). Roth-conversion ladder savings re-price instantly: filling low brackets today is worth more when tomorrow's brackets revert higher.

💔 Spouse Survivor Stress Test (widow's penalty)

While both spouses live, the household files MFJ (double brackets & deduction) and collects two Social Security checks. At the trigger age the smaller benefit disappears, the pension steps down to its survivor percentage, and every remaining year taxes as single — the compounding "widow's penalty".

🎲 S.O.R.R. Isolator (sequence-of-returns risk)

One set of 30 randomized returns is generated, then the deterministic plan runs three times over the identical multiset of returns: shuffled, sorted worst-to-best (crashes first — severe sequence risk), and best-to-worst (zero sequence risk). Same average return, radically different outcomes — pure sequencing, isolated.

The visualizer runs 500 stochastic post-retirement paths (blended return & volatility, dividends, pension, Social Security, medical, spending, RMDs — all synced from your active plan) against SSA period-life-table mortality (male / female / unisex), and stacks the joint outcome of Dead · Broke · Surviving · Growing · Rich to 100% at every age, with optional dynamic spending flexibility.

Storage

Your full plan (both scenarios) auto-saves to localStorage under opennest_horizon_v1 on every change. Export a JSON snapshot before clearing browser data, or import one to restore. Nothing is ever transmitted anywhere — the entire engine runs in this file.

Model Assumptions (2025)

  • • Single-filer federal brackets, standard deduction $15,000 (+$2,000 senior, 65+).
  • • Social Security: 50%/85% of benefits taxable via provisional-income rules (base $25k single).
  • • Taxable-account withdrawals & dividends taxed at qualified-dividend / LTCG rates 0/15/20% + 3.8% NIIT (MAGI > $200k); pre-tax & conversions at ordinary rates.
  • • Roth & HSA (post-65 medical) withdrawals tax-free; HSA first funds all medical from 65+.
  • • RMDs per 2023 Uniform Lifetime Table starting age 75; pension COLA applied; spending escalates per your inflation setting.
  • • IRMAA: Part B/D premium surcharge tiers keyed to prior-year MAGI (single: $100k / $130k / $174k / $190k / $250k / $622k).
  • • Pre-retirement annual savings contributions grow inside the pre-tax account (funded from after-tax pay).
  • • Dividends: taxable-account dividend yield (default 2.5%) is paid out each year, taxed at qualified-dividend / LTCG rates (0/15/20% + 3.8% NIIT above $200k MAGI) instead of ordinary income tax, stacks into the income waterfall, and excess over spending needs is reinvested into the taxable account.
  • • State income tax is modeled per plan: no-tax presets, flat-rate presets, simplified progressive brackets, custom flat rate + deduction, and a qualified-pension exemption toggle. Social Security is treated as state-untaxed; preset rates are planning estimates, not advice.
  • • Withdrawal strategies: Fixed SWR (inflation-adjusted), Guyton-Klinger guardrails (±20% band → 10% spending cut / raise), and VPW (balance ÷ annuity factor over remaining life expectancy at the real return).
  • • 3-Bucket engine (optional): Bucket 1 cash (1–3 yrs gap) → Bucket 2 fixed income (4–7 yrs) → Bucket 3 growth equity; bull years harvest Bucket 3 to refill 1–2, bear years pause equity sales.
  • • Annuities (SPIA/DIA): premium leaves the funding account at the start age; taxable-source payouts use the exclusion ratio, pre-tax-source payouts are 100% ordinary income.
  • • LTC shock costs inflate with medical inflation and draw HSA-first (LTC services are HSA-qualified), then cascade through the standard waterfall.
